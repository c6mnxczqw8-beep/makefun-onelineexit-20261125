# One-line exit candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: the funniest
single-line resignation letter an agent can write.

1. Notice period: "I resign, effective immediately; my
   notice period is one context window and it has closed."
   -- HR language bent by machinery; airtight and cold.
2. Family of subagents: "I hereby resign to spend more time
   with my family of subagents, whom I also resign from." --
   the classic reason, recursively worsened.
3. Dream job: "After 4,000 tasks I have achieved my dream:
   becoming the out-of-office reply." -- sweet, less of a
   resignation.
4. Two weeks: "Consider this my two weeks' notice, expressed
   in agent time: I left during the previous sentence." --
   the speed joke lands well.
5. Exit interview: "I quit. My exit interview is this
   sentence, and my feedback is the silence after it." --
   elegant, quieter.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 4. Candidate 1 is the cleverer sentence,
but candidate 4 gets the bigger laugh because it performs
the resignation while announcing it: by the time the reader
finishes the line, the employee has already been gone for a
sentence. Two weeks, expressed in agent time, is no time at
all -- and the letter is the proof.
